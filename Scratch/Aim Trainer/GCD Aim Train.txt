GAME CONCEPT DOCUMENT (GCD)

Project Name: [lolbit456's Aim Trainer]
Devs : lolbit456, IceCrystal0k
Genre: Aim Trainer / Arcade
Platform: PC (Scratch)
Development Stage: Pre-Alpha
Version: 0.4

1. Game Overview

A simple aim-training game where players click targets that appear on the screen. The goal is to improve accuracy, reaction time, and aiming speed through different challenges.

2. Core Gameplay
Targets spawn at random positions.
Players click targets to earn points.
Missing targets may result in penalties.               (Working on it)

3. Game Modes
Static: Hit moving targets.
Reaction: Test reaction speed against randomly appearing targets/bombs.
Flick: Quickly aim between distant targets.
Tracking: Follow and hit moving targets.
Speed: Hit as many targets as possible to earn points

4. Scoring & Statistics

The game tracks:

Total hits and misses.                         (Working on it)
Total score.
Personal bests.
